Installation

The lighthouse tool keeps a log of every ship that passes the island. Install it on the keeper's machine before the first watch, so the logbook is ready when the supply boat lands at dusk.

Each entry records the wind, the weather and the ship's name. The keeper reads them back in the morning and copies the important ones into the volume kept in the lamp room for the inspectors.

When the fog is thick, the tool rings the bell on a timer, and the keeper can sleep for an hour before he climbs the spiral stairs to check the great lens and trim the wick once more.
