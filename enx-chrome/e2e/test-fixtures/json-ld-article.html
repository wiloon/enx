World Harbour Weather

The old harbour was quiet for years after the last ferry left, and the sailor who kept the lighthouse watched the empty water every night. People in the town said the lights would never return to the port.

This spring a small group of volunteers repaired the lamps one by one. They carried glass and copper up the narrow stairs, and they painted the iron rails a bright and cheerful blue for the coming season.

When the first lamp was lit again, children ran along the pier to see it. The sailor stood at the top of the tower and waved to everyone, and the boats in the bay answered with a long and happy sound of horns.

Now the harbour is busy in the evenings. Fishermen tie their boats near the steps, visitors walk along the stone wall, and the light turns slowly over the dark water, as it did for many years before.
