An Essay Without Markup

Some personal sites put their essays straight into the page with no article element and no content class, just paragraphs one after the other. The writing is still an article, but no selector in the extension's list can find it, so learning mode falls back to the whole page and offers selection lookup only.

That fallback keeps the page usable, yet it loses click lookup, highlighting and reading statistics. Measuring how often it happens across real sites is the reason the evaluation tool exists at all, because a guess about coverage is no substitute for a number.

A reader who meets this page will still double-click a word to look it up, and the popover will open as it does everywhere else, but a single click on a word will reach the page's own handlers untouched.
